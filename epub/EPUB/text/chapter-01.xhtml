Understanding Breast Cancer

This chapter provides basic information about breast cancer and why awareness and early detection are important.

What is Breast Cancer?

Breast cancer occurs when abnormal cells in the breast grow and divide in an uncontrolled way.

Why is Awareness Important?

Being aware of changes in the breast can help a person recognise when they should seek advice from a healthcare professional.

Important Information

Finding a change does not necessarily mean that a person has breast cancer. A healthcare professional should assess any concerning change.
