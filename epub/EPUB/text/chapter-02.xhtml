Symptoms of Breast Cancer

Symptoms of Breast Cancer in Women

Changes in Breast Size

One breast may suddenly change in size or shape.

Breast or nipple pain

The breast or nipple may feel pain, sore or uncomfortable.

Changes in skin texture

Changes in skin texture

The skin may become rough, thick or look like an orange peel.

Dimpling

Small dents or dimple-like dips may appear on the breast.

Lymph node changes

Lumps or swelling may appear under the skin.

Nipple discharge

Liquid may come out of the nipple when the person is not bleeding.

Retracted or inverted nipple

The nipple may suddenly turn or pull inward

Changes in skin colour

The breast skin may become red, darker or look bruised.

Swelling

Part or all the breasts may become swollen or feel bigger.

These symptoms do not always mean that a person has breast cancer. However, any unusual breast change should be checked by a healthcare professional

Symptoms of Breast Cancer in Men

Although breast cancer is much less common in men, unusual changes should still be checked by a healthcare professional.

Men can experience most of the same symptoms as women, including:

  • A lump or swelling in the chest or armpit
  • Changes in the skin
  • Nipple discharge
  • A nipple that turns inward
  • Pain or tenderness
  • Changes in the size or shape of the chest
  • A rash or sore around the nipple

For both men and women, changes do not always mean that one has breast cancer, as they can have other causes. However, any new, unusual or persistent breast change should be checked by a healthcare professional.
