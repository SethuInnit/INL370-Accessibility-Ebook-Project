Breast Cancer Signs and Symptoms

Knowing the possible signs and symptoms of breast cancer can help people recognise changes in their bodies and know when to seek advice from a healthcare professional.

Possible Changes to Look For

Changes in the breast or chest area can include:

  • A new lump or swelling in the breast or armpit.
  • A change in the size or shape of the breast.
  • A change in the appearance of the skin.
  • A change in the appearance of the nipple.
  • Unusual discharge from the nipple.
  • Persistent pain or discomfort in the breast or chest area.

Important to Remember

These changes do not necessarily mean that a person has breast cancer. Many breast changes can have other causes.

If you notice a new or unusual change that concerns you, speak to a healthcare professional so that it can be assessed.
