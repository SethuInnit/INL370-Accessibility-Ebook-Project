Ukuwohloka komdlavuza

Uyini umdlavuza

Umdlavuza yisifo esenzeka lapho amanye amaseli emzimbeni eqala ukukhula futhi ahlukane ngendlela engalawuleki. Ngokuvamile, amaseli ayakhula, ahlukane futhi afe ngenqubo ehlelekile. Umdlavuza ungavela lapho le nqubo iba engajwayelekile. Amaseli angajwayelekile angakha inqwaba ebizwa ngokuthi isimila. Ezinye izimila azinawo umdlavuza, kanti izimila ezinomdlavuza zingahlasela izicubu eziseduze futhi zingasakazekela kwezinye izingxenye zomzimba.

Uqala kanjani umdlavuza?

Umdlavuza ungaqala lapho kwenzeka izinguquko emiyalweni yamaseli, isibonelo i-DNA engaphakathi kweseli iba noshintsho noma yonakale., okwenza ukuthi akhule lapho kungafanele.

Ngokuhamba kwesikhathi, la maseli angajwayelekile angakha isimila. Ezinye izimila azinabungozi, kanti izimila ezinomdlavuza zingakhula ziye ezicutshini eziseduze.

Ingabe umdlavuza ungasakazeka?

Yebo. Eminye imidlavuza ingasakazeka isuke endaweni yayo yokuqala iye kwezinye izingxenye zomzimba.

Le nqubo ibizwa ngokuthi metastasis.

Lapho umdlavuza usakazeka, ungathinta ezinye izitho futhi wenze ukwelashwa kube nzima kakhulu.

Akuyona yonke imidlavuza esakazekayo, ikakhulukazi lapho itholakala futhi yelashwa kusenesikhathi.

Uyini umdlavuza webele?

Umdlavuza webele uwuhlobo lomdlavuza oluqala kumaseli ebele.

Ungavela kwabesifazane nakwabesilisa, nakuba uvame kakhulu kwabesifazane.

Umdlavuza webele ungathinta izingxenye ezahlukene zebele futhi ungakhula ngezindlela ezahlukene.

Yiziphi izinhlobo ezivamile zomdlavuza webele?

Kunezinhlobo ezahlukene zomdlavuza webele. Ezinye ezivamile zihlanganisa:

Invasive ductal carcinoma (IDC): Uhlobo oluvamile lomdlavuza webele oluqala emigudwini yobisi futhi lusakazekele ezicutshini zebele eziseduze (Cancer.gov, 2025).

Invasive lobular carcinoma (ILC): Uhlobo lomdlavuza webele oluqala ezindlaleni ezikhiqiza ubisi (ama-lobules) futhi lusakazekele ezicutshini eziseduze (Cancer.gov, 2025).

Inflammatory breast cancer: Uhlobo olungavamile nolukhula ngokushesha lomdlavuza webele olungenza ibele libukeke libomvu, livuvukele futhi livuvuke (Cancer.gov, 2025).

Triple-negative breast cancer: Uhlobo lomdlavuza webele olungenawo ama-receptor amathathu avamile noma amaprotheni amanye umdlavuza webele owasebenzisayo ukuze ukhule (Cancer.gov, 2025).

Metastatic breast cancer: Umdlavuza webele osakazeke usuka ebeleni waya kwezinye izingxenye zomzimba futhi waziwa nangokuthi Stage 4 breast cancer (Cancer.gov, 2025).

Ductal carcinoma in situ (DCIS): Uhlobo lokuqala lomdlavuza webele oluhlala ngaphakathi kwemigudu yobisi futhi lungasakazekanga ezicutshini zebele ezizungezile (Cancer.gov, 2025).

Paget disease of the breast: Uhlobo olungavamile lomdlavuza webele oluthinta isikhumba sengono kanye nendawo emnyama ezungezile (areola) (Cancer.gov, 2025).

Phyllodes tumour: Isimila sebele esingavamile esikhula ezicutshini ezixhumanisayo, futhi izimila eziningi ze-phyllodes azinawo umdlavuza (Cancer.gov, 2025).

Lobular carcinoma in situ (LCIS): Isimo lapho amaseli angajwayelekile akhula ezindlaleni ezikhiqiza ubisi futhi andise ingozi yokuba nomdlavuza webele esikhathini esizayo (Cancer.gov, 2025).

Uchwepheshe wezempilo anganquma uhlobo oluthile ngokusebenzisa ukuhlolwa kwezokwelapha.

Ngabe amadoda angaba nomdlavuza webele?

Yebo. Amadoda nawo angaba nomdlavuza webele.

Nakuba umdlavuza webele ungavamile kakhulu emadodeni, nawo angaba nalesi sifo ngoba amadoda nawo anenani elincane lezicubu zebele.

Ngakho-ke amadoda akufanele azibe izinguquko ezingajwayelekile ebeleni noma endaweni yesifuba.

Yiziphi izici eziyinhloko zobungozi?

Isici sobungozi yinto engandisa amathuba omuntu okuba nesifo.

Ezinye izici zobungozi zomdlavuza webele zihlanganisa:

  • Ukukhula ngeminyaka.
  • Umlando womndeni womdlavuza webele.
  • Ezinye izinguquko zofuzo ezizuzwe njengefa.
  • Ezinye izici zamahomoni.
  • Ezinye izici zendlela yokuphila nezemvelo.

Ukuba nesici sobungozi akusho ukuthi umuntu uzoba nomdlavuza.

Kungani ukutholakala kusenesikhathi kubalulekile?

Ukuthola umdlavuza webele kusenesikhathi kunganikeza umuntu ithuba elingcono lokuthola ukwelashwa ngokushesha.

Yingakho kubalulekile:

  • Ukwazi ukuthi yini evamile emzimbeni wakho.
  • Ukunaka izinguquko ezingajwayelekile.
  • Ukulandela izeluleko ezinconyiwe zokuhlolwa.
  • Ukufuna usizo lochwepheshe bezempilo lapho ukhathazekile.

Ukuqwashisa kusenesikhathi kungaholela ekuthathweni kwezinyathelo kusenesikhathi.
