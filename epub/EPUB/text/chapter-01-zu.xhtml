Ukuqonda Umdlavuza Webele

Lesi sahluko sinikeza ulwazi oluyisisekelo ngomdlavuza webele nokuthi kungani ukuqwashisa nokutholwa kusenesikhathi kubalulekile.

Uyini Umdlavuza Webele?

Umdlavuza webele uba khona lapho amaseli angajwayelekile ebeleni ekhula futhi ehlukana ngendlela engalawuleki.

Kungani Ukuqwashisa Kubalulekile?

Ukwazi izinguquko ezenzeka ebeleni kungasiza umuntu ukuba abone ukuthi kufanele afune usizo noma iseluleko kuchwepheshe wezempilo.

Ulwazi Olubalulekile

Ukuthola ushintsho akusho ukuthi umuntu unomdlavuza webele. Uchwepheshe wezempilo kufanele ahlole noma yiluphi ushintsho olukhathazayo.
